Every request Cloudflare saw is here; the filters choose what you look at and the counts follow the filters. "People" means a real browser user agent; "not people" means scanners, uptime probes, link unfurlers and headless test runs. Launches are the anonymous count the page sends when someone presses Enter and are never filtered. Accounts and sign-ins come from the database. Cloudflare samples analytics on very busy days, so large numbers are estimates.
