tell me everything about
Open the page

A home page that finishes your prompt for you.

Most of us ask the same kinds of questions all day. Tell me everything about a person, a company, a word you just heard. Explain something simply. What is the latest on a topic. The question changes, the shape of it does not, and typing the shape every time is the part that gets old.

This page keeps the shape. Pick a prompt, type the topic, press Enter. It opens, already asked, in Google AI Mode, Google Search, Perplexity, ChatGPT, Claude, Grok or DuckDuckGo. Set it as your browser's home page and it is there every time you open a window.

How it works

  • Keep up to six prompts. Each has a short name, the wording, and the place it opens. Write {} where the topic goes, or leave it out and the topic is added at the end.
  • The page builds the final sentence and hands it to the engine as a normal search link. Nothing is generated here and nothing is answered here. The engine you chose does that, on its own site, under your own account there.
  • There is no feed, no history and no suggestions. It is a text box that already knows what you were going to say.

What it keeps

Your prompts and settings live in this browser, in its local storage. The topics you type go straight from your browser to the engine; they never pass through this site. When you launch, the page sends one anonymous count that says which engine was used, so the maker can tell whether anyone is using the page at all. That count carries no topic, no identifier and no cookie.

Signing in sets one cookie, which keeps you signed in and does nothing else.

Signing in is optional and exists for one reason: so your prompts follow you to another browser or computer. It uses a link sent to your email, with no password. The server then stores your email address, a session, and one small record of your prompts and settings. That is the whole list. Delete account in the Prompts sheet removes all of it at once. The prompts in the browser you are using stay.
